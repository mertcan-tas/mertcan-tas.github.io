## Overview

Hexpane replaces Chrome's new-tab page with a calm, frosted-glass launchpad. Your shortcuts sit on gradient backgrounds, organized into swipeable spaces and folders, with instant search across everything. Beyond bookmarks, a space can also be a full-screen mind map, so quick thoughts and plans live right where you start your day.

## Features

- **Spaces** — swipeable pages of shortcuts, each with its own gradient background.
- **Folders & drag-and-drop** — organize shortcuts the way you think.
- **Instant search** — a launchpad-style filter across every space.
- **Mind maps** — full-screen, XMind-style canvases as their own spaces: colored branches, pan/zoom and keyboard-first editing.
- **Glass design** — a minimal, frosted aesthetic that stays legible over any wallpaper.
- **Local-first** — everything is stored on your device, with backup export/import.

## Stack

- **Vue 3 + Pinia** for the UI and state, with **Vue Router** and **vue-i18n** (TR/EN).
- **Tailwind CSS 4** for the frosted-glass design system.
- **Vite** build, packaged as a Chrome (Manifest V3) new-tab extension.
